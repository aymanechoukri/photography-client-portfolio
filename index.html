    01 --- ABOUT ---

    Light, silence and the in-between moments.

    I’m Oussama, a photographer based in Safi, drawn to the quiet poetry of everyday life. For nearly a decade I’ve travelled with a camera to tell stories of people, places and the fleeting light that connects them. My work blends documentary honesty with a cinematic eye — whether it’s an intimate wedding, a fashion editorial or a remote landscape at the edge of the world.
